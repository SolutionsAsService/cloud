# Critic: Formalist (Arnheim) - R1

**Anchor used:** Arnheim, Art and Visual Perception: balance, structural skeleton, visual weight, direction, figure and ground.

**Observed**
- 1280: the cloud sits on the vertical axis (about x=640) inside a grey frame. It is a dense blue outline with a large pale centre, so figure/ground is clear. Weight is symmetric: five annotations left, five right, in black, red, green and blue. "the Internet" is dead centre. Two boxes at the bottom corners with arcs up to the cloud give a stable triangular skeleton.
- The h1 is heavy and top-left. The lede sits beside it. The board is the large, centred mass below, so left-heavy header over a centred figure.
- 375: the cloud shrinks to about 260 px wide in a 340 px frame. The numerals are tiny, with no annotations and no computer/server doodle. A two-column key below holds more text mass than the cloud.

**Findings**
1. [defect] At 375 the figure loses its skeleton. The cloud is a small, weakly weighted pale shape with a hollow centre. The key beneath (about 230 px tall) outweighs it, so the graphic reads as a list with an icon above it. The first screen's visual centre of gravity is the text, not the cloud.
2. [defect] At 1280 the annotation lines are thin, short and pale. The pointers (e.g. 1 and 6 at the top) are weaker than the lobe outline, so label-to-lobe connection depends on proximity, not on a strong directional vector.
3. [claim] The mirrored 5+5 layout is balanced to the point of stasis. The red and blue sit in nearly mirrored positions, which gives no tension or lead direction. Only the bottom arcs add motion.
4. [claim] The h1 and lede sit off-axis against the centred board, which leaves a small unresolved left-heavy tension. The lede is small beside the h1 and the board.
5. [premise] Colour is used as a four-way category on the numerals and labels. It carries no obvious meaning, so it adds noise to the weight distribution.

**Verdict:** OBJECT (finding 1)

**Requested change:** At 375, enlarge the cloud to full frame width, with bolder lobes and larger numerals. Cut the key's height so the cloud stays the dominant weight. At 1280, thicken the leader lines to match the stroke weight of the cloud.
